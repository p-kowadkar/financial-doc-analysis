PART I

Item 1. Business

Company Overview
Apple Inc. designs, manufactures and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables and accessories, and sells a variety of related services. The Company's fiscal year is the 52 or 53-week period that ends on the last Saturday of September.

Products
iPhone: The Company's line of smartphones based on its iOS operating system. iPhone revenue for fiscal 2023 was $200.6 billion, representing 52% of total net sales.

Mac: The Company's line of personal computers based on its macOS operating system. Mac revenue for fiscal 2023 was $29.4 billion.

iPad: The Company's line of multi-purpose tablets based on its iPadOS operating system. iPad revenue for fiscal 2023 was $28.3 billion.

Wearables, Home and Accessories: This category includes AirPods, Apple TV, Apple Watch, Beats products, HomePod, iPod touch and accessories. Revenue for fiscal 2023 was $39.8 billion.

Services: The Company's services include advertising, AppleCare, cloud services, digital content and payment services. Services revenue for fiscal 2023 was $85.2 billion.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

The following risk factors may materially affect the Company's business, financial condition and results of operations:

Global Economic Conditions
The Company's operations and performance depend significantly on global and regional economic conditions. Adverse economic conditions can reduce demand for the Company's products and services.

Competition
The markets for the Company's products and services are highly competitive. The Company faces substantial competition in all areas of its business.

Supply Chain
The Company depends on third-party suppliers for components and manufacturing. Disruptions to the supply chain could materially adversely affect the Company's business.

Technology Risks
The Company's business depends on continued innovation and the development of new products and services. Failure to innovate could harm the Company's competitive position.

Cybersecurity and Privacy
The Company faces cybersecurity threats that could result in unauthorized access to sensitive information or disruption of operations.

Item 2. Properties

The Company's headquarters are located in Cupertino, California. The Company owns or leases facilities in various locations worldwide for research and development, manufacturing, warehousing, distribution and administrative purposes.

Item 3. Legal Proceedings

The Company is subject to various legal proceedings and claims that arise in the ordinary course of business.

Item 5. Market for Registrant's Common Equity and Related Stockholder Matters

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Fiscal Year 2023 Performance
Total net sales for fiscal 2023 were $383.3 billion, a decrease of 3% compared to fiscal 2022. The decrease was primarily due to foreign exchange headwinds and challenging macroeconomic conditions.

Net income for fiscal 2023 was $97.0 billion, compared to $99.8 billion in fiscal 2022. Diluted earnings per share were $6.16 in fiscal 2023, compared to $6.11 in fiscal 2022.

Revenue by Product Category:
- iPhone: $200.6 billion (52% of total revenue)
- Services: $85.2 billion (22% of total revenue)
- Wearables, Home and Accessories: $39.8 billion (10% of total revenue)
- Mac: $29.4 billion (8% of total revenue)
- iPad: $28.3 billion (7% of total revenue)

Geographic Revenue:
- Americas: $162.6 billion
- Europe: $94.3 billion
- Greater China: $72.6 billion
- Japan: $24.9 billion
- Rest of Asia Pacific: $28.9 billion

Cash and Cash Equivalents
As of September 30, 2023, the Company had cash and cash equivalents of $29.5 billion and marketable securities of $148.1 billion.

Research and Development
Research and development expenses were $29.9 billion in fiscal 2023, representing 7.8% of total net sales.
