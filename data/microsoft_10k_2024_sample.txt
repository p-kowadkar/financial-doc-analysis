PART I

Item 1. Business

Company Overview
Microsoft Corporation is a technology company whose mission is to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more. We strive to create local opportunity, growth, and impact in every country around the world.

Products and Services
Our products include operating systems, cross-device productivity applications, server applications, business solution applications, desktop and server management tools, software development tools, and video games. We also design and sell devices, including PCs, tablets, gaming and entertainment consoles, other intelligent devices, and related accessories.

Our segments are:
- Productivity and Business Processes: Office, LinkedIn, and Dynamics
- Intelligent Cloud: Server products and cloud services, including Azure and other cloud services
- More Personal Computing: Windows, devices, gaming, and search

Item 1A. Risk Factors

The following risk factors may materially affect our business, financial condition and results of operations:

Intense Competition
Our businesses face intense competition. We compete with companies that develop, license, or sell competing software, services, and devices.

Evolving Technology Landscape
The computing industry is continuously evolving. Our success depends on our ability to anticipate technology trends and develop innovative products and services.

Cybersecurity and Privacy Risks
Cyberattacks and security vulnerabilities could lead to reduced revenue, increased costs, liability claims, or harm to our reputation and competitive position.

Regulatory Risks
Our business is subject to complex and evolving U.S. and foreign laws and regulations regarding privacy, data protection, content, competition, and other matters.

Global Operations
Our business depends on our ability to attract and retain talented employees. We operate globally and our operations in certain parts of the world are subject to risks of doing business internationally.

Item 2. Properties

Our corporate headquarters are located in Redmond, Washington. We have significant operations in various locations worldwide for research and development, manufacturing, warehousing, distribution, and administrative purposes.

Item 3. Legal Proceedings

We are subject to various legal proceedings and claims that arise in the ordinary course of business.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Fiscal Year 2024 Performance
Total revenue for fiscal 2024 was $245.1 billion, an increase of 15% compared to fiscal 2023. The increase was primarily driven by growth in our cloud business, particularly Azure and other cloud services.

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $87.3 billion, compared to $72.4 billion in fiscal 2023. Diluted earnings per share were $11.72 in fiscal 2024, compared to $9.68 in fiscal 2023.

Revenue by Segment:
- Productivity and Business Processes: $75.3 billion (31% of total revenue)
- Intelligent Cloud: $106.4 billion (43% of total revenue)
- More Personal Computing: $63.4 billion (26% of total revenue)

Cash and Cash Equivalents
As of June 30, 2024, we had cash and cash equivalents of $34.7 billion and short-term investments of $71.2 billion.

Research and Development
Research and development expenses were $28.7 billion in fiscal 2024, representing 11.7% of total revenue.
